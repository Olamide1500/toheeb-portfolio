About me

Hi, I’m Toheeb.

I’m a Product Designer based in Lagos, Nigeria, with a background in Economics and an interest in building digital products that solve everyday problems.

Studying Economics taught me that people don’t always make decisions based on what’s best for them. Sometimes, they choose what feels easier, faster, or more convenient. That perspective shapes how I design: I try to understand why people behave the way they do, then make the right choice the easy one.

So far I’ve built my practice through my own projects: a check-in tool people actually use, a savings app that makes it hard to break your own plan, and redesigns of screens Nigerians deal with every day.

I’m open to remote roles and to working with teams who care about the people using what they build.

How I think

How Economics shapes my design

I design for how people really behave.

When a tool is confusing, people stop using it.

So I make the main action obvious.

Plans break when real life happens.

So I design for the moment things go wrong.

People trust what they understand.

So I show the reason behind every result.
