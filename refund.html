Refund tracker

Where’s my money?

A failed-transfer tracker inside a bank app. It answers the question before you have to ask it: is my money safe, when is it back, and should I resend?

ForBank app users waiting on a failed transfer

The short version

Problem
When a transfer fails, the balance drops and nothing says if or when the money will come back. People resend and risk a double debit, or call and wait.
What I did
Looked at complaint data, news reports and a short survey, explored 3 Home directions, then designed the refund journey from lock screen to report, with both endings.
Outcome
A 7-step flow · status in 4 places · report in 3 taps with 0 forms. Concept, not yet tested, and not affiliated with any bank.

01 · The problem

The balance drops. Then nothing.

A transfer of mine failed, and I had no idea if or when the money would come back. Customers describe the same thing: often the only sign is the balance drop. There’s no timeline, no warning not to resend, and reporting it means calls and follow-ups.

How might we

Tell people their money is safe before they panic, show when it will be back, and make reporting easy only if it’s late?

02 · Why it matters

I’ve been there. So have a lot of people.

This started with my own failed transfer. Then I looked at how common it is, using public reports rather than interviews.

03 · Key decisions

Three choices that answer before you ask.

01

Tell them first

The news arrives on the lock screen with a live countdown, before panic sets in. The same status also shows on Home, in history and in the transfer details.

Answers: silence causes panic
02

One smart tile on Home

I explored 3 Home directions. In the chosen one, a single tile shows the most useful thing right now: a problem beats a bill that’s due, which beats Send again.

Answers: where it lives
03

“Don’t resend yet”

A clear timeline (Debited → Failed → Refunding) plus a warning not to resend until the money is back, because retrying can debit you twice.

Answers: resending is risky

04 · Two endings

Back by 18:10, or report in 3 taps.

At 18:08 the money is back: the tracker turns green and Send again is safe. If it’s still not back at 18:10, Report unlocks on its own. Details attach automatically, and you get a case number with what happens next. If the network can’t give a time, the countdown is replaced by the bank’s published reversal window. No fake timers.

05 · Visual design

Calm colours for a stressful moment.

Every colour has one job, so status reads at a glance: amber means it’s coming, clay means act, sage means it’s done. Pine and cream keep the rest of the screen quiet.

08 · What I learned

What this project taught me.

Fix it where people look

Putting the status on the lock screen, Home and history beat building a new screen.

Timing is the feature

The same information helps more when it arrives before people go looking.

No fake timers

When there’s no reliable time, honesty beats a countdown.

What I’d test next

5 people who’ve had a failed transfer: can 4 of 5 say when the money is back and whether to resend?
